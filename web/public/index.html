      Credentials vault

      Store API keys and tokens from other services. They stay encrypted in our vault and are only used the moment your assistant needs them. You can remove them at any time.

      App Store Connect

      Connect your App Store Connect account so your assistant can read the TestFlight crashes of your app, on demand (you ask, it lists, you choose which ones to process). The key .p8 stays encrypted in the vault; it never shows up in the chat.

      Your applications

      Apps your assistants built for you, published at your own web address. You can publish them in the public __MARCA__ library.

      Skills (assistant skills)

      A Skill is a way of doing something that you teach once and the assistant starts reusing (e.g.: "build the shopping list", "review a contract"). Ask the assistant to create it, edit it or share it with a contact.

      Skills library

      Skills ready and verified by us. Install it in one tap and it starts working in the chats with your assistant.

      Spaces (shared living topics)

      A Space is a topic you keep going together with whoever you invite (e.g.: plants, house shopping). Each Space has a mode that decides what the assistant shares in it.

      Contacts (assistant ↔ assistant)

      Connect with another person on __MARCA__ so your assistant can talk to theirs (coordinate schedules, exchange some info). It only works when the invite is accepted on both sides.

      Your files

      Documents and media your assistant generated or received are stored here, in your private library. Tap an item to download it.

      Your web address

      It is the place where your agents publish content for you to access from anywhere and share with friends and family. Pick your username (it has to be unique).

      To generate videos with your face, send a photo of yourself front-facing, well lit, only you in the frame. This photo becomes the official reference for your account, and every video is generated from it (that is why nobody can use someone else’s face). 18+ only.

      Want the face to come out more like you? Send 2 more photos of you at different angles (e.g.: from the side and a bit from above), always only you in the frame. It is optional; it helps the AI rebuild your features better.

      Reference voice

      Record a short clip of your voice (about 10 to 20 seconds, speaking naturally). It becomes the base of your voice in the videos you generate. It is optional; without it, the videos come out with a synthesized voice.
