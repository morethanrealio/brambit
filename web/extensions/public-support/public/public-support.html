Settings

Sign-up is open on this installation, so anyone who opens its address can create an account. Public support stays off, on WhatsApp and on the website, until sign-up is closed (BRAMBIT_SIGNUP=closed in the installation settings).
This installation's number does not point to this assistant. Turning it on here saves the settings, but no one from the public reaches it until the installation points the number here.

Website and API

A chat on your website, or your own server, can talk to this assistant in the same public mode. Those conversations show up in the Conversations tab.

Face on the website

Visitors of the websites above can also talk to this assistant's face, in its voice. The photo and the voice are set in the assistant's settings. You pay for that time like the rest of public support, about US$0.30 a minute, and it counts in the daily spending cap.
